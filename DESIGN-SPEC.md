# Wings of Honor — art and game design spec

## Creative direction

Premium-feeling, readable WWII arcade flight combat. Metal fighters, cinematic sky, and restrained military UI. Adventurous and punchy rather than grim or simulator-heavy.

## Palette

- Night navy: `#101820`
- Olive drab: `#3D4A32`
- Aluminum: `#8A9084`
- Brass gold: `#C9A24A`
- Sky blue: `#7899A4`
- Oxide red: `#793E34`
- Warm ivory: `#E8E4D6`

## Type

Display: Barlow Condensed, 700–800 weight, uppercase, tight leading. Body/UI: DM Sans, 400–700.

## Aircraft material language

Metal skin is riveted and slightly oily; desert schemes are matte sand; RAF fighters wear green-and-earth camouflage. National markings should be legible at distance. Exaggerate silhouette and motion slightly for readability on phone screens. Current plane meshes are procedural placeholders.

## Campaign

Five theatres, twenty missions each: Battle of Britain, Desert Storm, Fortress Europe, Pacific Thunder, Final Offensive.
