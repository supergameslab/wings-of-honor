        TOP PILOTS
          HOW TO FLY

          Easy as pie.

          CLIMB / DIVE
          W or ↑ pulls the nose up. S or ↓ points it down. Aim, then fire.

          TURN
          A / ← and D / → tilt the wings. The plane turns the way you lean.

          FIRE
          Hold SPACE or FIRE. Named aces like Hartmann take more hits and are worth extra points.

          BOMBS & MAP
          On bomb missions, put the diamond on the target and press B. Red dots on the little map are planes. Gold is stuff on the ground.

          COCKPIT
          Press C to sit inside the plane. Press it again to fly behind it.

          HANGAR
          Finish campaign missions to unlock more planes.

          HOME FIELD
          On later campaign jobs, hold FIRE on the grass to taxi, then press T when the HUD says TAKE OFF. Line up with the sheds, wait for LAND, press L. Tanks and bombs fill up. Cadet, Quick Mission, and Hunt skip this.
